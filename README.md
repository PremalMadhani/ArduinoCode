# ArduinoCode
In myINS folder, the insAll.ion contains the low pass filter, Complimentary filter and Kalman filter for sensor fusion to reduce noise of sensors and get optimal estimates of orientation angles which will help detect the motion more accurately for Priyan's use of accelerometers for motion detection feature for his drone project. Also, instead of Euler angles, quaternions are used which give accurate information for all orientation and motions since 
quaternions do not have Gimbal lock. This can be tested with the code when you raise the board with pitch angle of 90degs. Looking at the board along the 'star' length, the +X axis goes from 9 axis to BNO055 letter along the start symbol length. The +Y axis goes along 9 axis breath from start symbol to Vin. Rotation around +X axis counterclockwise is roll (phi), rotation along +Y axis in counterclockwise is pitch (theta), and rotating about +Z axis counterclockwise (which is vertical up at 9 axis and start symbol intersection) is yaw (psi).

We can compare low pass filtered phi and theta angles, with complimentary filtered angles and Kalman filtered angles. You will see that Kalman and complimentary follow the acceleration very well and have no lag and are less noisy. In some cases when acceleration is very high, or gyro is biased, Kalman filter will perform well compared to Complimentary filter, since it estimates the gyro bias state.

So instead of low pass filter, Priyan can use complimentary or Kalman filter to detect motion more accurately and feed it to his ML algorithm to detect motion and control the drone. Quaternions can also be used as output from the BNO055 chip, since they are processed by internal Kalman filter and makes use of gyros and magnetometers to compensate for accelerometer errors. This can be compared with your own Kalman filter, which is done in the code.

Taking it a step further, In myGPS folder, there is gpsPlusIns which combines INS with GPS meas to get more accurate positions at a higher rate than what GPS can provide. Here complimentary filter is used to fuse the two. INS is less noisy but its position drifts over time, GPS is more noisy but has steady position. When going inside a tunnel, ir indoors, GPS will stip producing fixes, but INS will keep giving fixes during GPS outage. This is very helpful in real life situations. In gpsPlusInsKalman, in addition to complimentary filter, Kalman filter is included and you can see that without Kalman filter complimentary filter can drift if gyros are biased.
